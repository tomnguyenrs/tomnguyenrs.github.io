I am a PhD student in Information Management and Finance at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), Taiwan. My research spans corporate finance, banking, and corporate governance, as well as the evolution of finance research itself. My work has been published in the _Review of Quantitative Finance and Accounting_ and the _Journal of Economic Surveys_, and I serve as a reviewer for the _Pacific-Basin Finance Journal_ and _Humanities and Social Sciences Communications_.

**Research interests:** Corporate Finance · Banking · Corporate Governance · Bibliometrics of Finance Research

**Education**

- PhD, Information Management and Finance — National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, 2024–present
- MS, Global Finance (Valedictorian) — National Chung Cheng University, 2022–2024
- BSc, Biotechnology — Can Tho University, 2017–2022
